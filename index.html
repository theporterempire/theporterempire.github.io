04 • THE EMPIRE STANDARD

Strong standards. Open doors.

These standards are not about perfection. They are about protecting the culture, the people, the vision, and the future we are trying to build.

01. Guard Your Tongue Speak life. No tearing people down, belittling, rumors, or destructive speech.
02. Respect Everyone Disagree without disrespect.
03. No Drama No unnecessary conflict, gossip, rumors, or division. Address problems directly or with leadership.
04. Take Responsibility Own your decisions, acknowledge mistakes, learn, correct, and move forward. Excuses don't build empires. Accountability does.
05. Be Willing to Learn Ask. Listen. Study. Practice. Accept correction.
06. Show Up Respect commitments and time. Communicate when circumstances change.
07. Build, Don't Destroy Bring solutions and help improve what is around you.
08. Keep Your Character Do the right thing even when nobody is watching.
09. No Jealousy Celebrate progress and learn from other people's success.
10. Protect the Empire Protect the culture, reputation, relationships, property, opportunities, and trust.
11. Work Hard, Work Wise Combine effort with preparation, patience, strategy, discipline, and learning.
12. Leave People Better Always ask: “Did I make this better?”
“Speak life. Respect people. Control your emotions. Take responsibility. Stay teachable. Keep your word. Work with purpose. Celebrate growth. Protect the vision. Give back. Keep God close. And never stop becoming better.”

“Finally, brethren, whatsoever things are true, whatsoever things are honest, whatsoever things are just, whatsoever things are pure, whatsoever things are lovely, whatsoever things are of good report; if there be any virtue, and if there be any praise, think on these things.” — Philippians 4:8
