04 • THE EMPIRE STANDARD

Strong standards. Open doors.

These standards are not about perfection. They are about protecting the culture, the people, the vision, and the future we are trying to build.

01 • Guard Your Tongue Speak life. No tearing people down, belittling others, spreading rumors, or using words to destroy yourself or someone else. “Death and life are in the power of the tongue.” — Proverbs 18:21
02 • Respect Everyone You can disagree without disrespect. Treat people with dignity, regardless of position, background, success, or status.
03 • No Drama No unnecessary conflict, gossip, rumors, or division. When there is a problem, address it directly, respectfully, and responsibly.
04 • Take Responsibility Own your decisions. If you make a mistake, acknowledge it, learn from it, correct it, and move forward. Excuses do not build empires.
05 • Be Willing to Learn Ask questions. Listen. Study. Practice. Accept correction. Nobody starts knowing everything, and nobody grows by pretending they do.
06 • Show Up Respect commitments, time, and opportunities. If circumstances change, communicate. Your word should mean something.
07 • Build, Don't Destroy Bring solutions. If something can be better, help make it better. Criticism without contribution is not leadership.
08 • Character When Nobody Is Watching Do what is right even without applause, attention, or recognition. Character is who you are when there is no audience.
09 • No Jealousy Celebrate progress. Learn from other people's wins. Their success does not remove your opportunity to grow.
10 • Protect the Empire Protect the culture, reputation, relationships, opportunities, property, and trust that people place in the Empire.
11 • Work Hard, Work Wise Effort matters. So do preparation, patience, strategy, discipline, and continuous learning.
12 • Leave People Better Wherever you go, ask yourself: “Did I make this better?” That question should shape how we lead, work, and treat people.

Speak life. Respect people. Control your emotions. Take responsibility. Stay teachable. Keep your word. Work with purpose. Celebrate growth. Protect the vision. Give back. Keep God close. And never stop becoming better.

“Whatsoever things are true... honest... just... pure... lovely... and of good report; think on these things.” — Philippians 4:8
